## 🌟 Join Nick Fang's Lab as an RPg Student!

We are currently inviting applications from motivated and talented individuals to join our team as **Research Postgraduate (RPg) students** for 2026/27 admission!

Our group focuses on multiple disciplines including but not limited to **ultrasound**, **nanophotonics**, **soft materials**, **energy materials**, **quantum materials**​, **microfludics**, tackling fundamental challenges and facilitating technology transfer for impactful real-world applications.

As part of our team, you'll collaborate in an interdisciplinary, dynamic, and supportive environment that fosters curiosity, rigor, and creativity.

### 🎓 Scholarship Opportunity

Outstanding full-time PhD applicants may be eligible for the **Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme**, which offers:

- ✅ Cash Award to support research and living expenses
- ✅ Waiver of composition fees for the whole normative study period
- ✅ Research support and travel funding  
- ✅ A guaranteed hall place in Year 1, with possibility of renewal

🔗 [Check more details about the fellowship](https://gradsch.hku.hk/prospective_students/fees_scholarships_and_financial_support/hong_kong_phd_fellowship_scheme)

HKU also offers other scholarships:
- HKU Presidential PhD Scholar Programme 🔗 [Check more details](https://gradsch.hku.hk/prospective_students/fees_scholarships_and_financial_support/hku_presidential_phd_scholar_programme)
- Postgraduate Scholarships for full-time PhD and MPhil students 🔗 [Check more detials](https://gradsch.hku.hk/prospective_students/fees_scholarships_and_financial_support/postgraduate_scholarships)
